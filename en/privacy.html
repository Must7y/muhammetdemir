Privacy and cookies

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This notice covers the personal website at https://must7y.github.io/muhammetdemir/. It does not cover mobile applications mentioned on the site.

1. Website operator and contact

The site is operated by Muhammet Demir. You can send website inquiries or requests about your rights under Türkiye's KVKK via the contact form. Please describe your request in the message; further verification may be needed for certain privacy requests.

2. Visits and cookies

This is a static website hosted by GitHub Pages. The website's own code does not set marketing or analytics cookies, track visitor behavior, or store language preferences in browser localStorage. GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses to operate and secure the service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

3. Contact form data

The form collects the name, email address and message you choose to submit. Formspree, Inc. may also process technical details such as IP address, browser information and submission time to prevent spam and protect the service. This information is used to deliver and respond to your message, protect against abuse and follow up on inquiries; it is not used by the website operator to build marketing lists. Please avoid sending sensitive data.

Depending on the circumstances, responding to inquiries may rely on legitimate interests under Article 5(2)(f) of Türkiye's KVKK. Other legal grounds may apply depending on the nature of an individual message. Reading this notice does not itself constitute consent.

4. Providers and international transfers

GitHub Pages hosts the website. Form submissions go to Formspree, may be stored in the Formspree inbox, and may be forwarded to the email recipient configured in the form account. Formspree states that it hosts its services in the United States, so submitting a message may transfer your personal data outside Türkiye. Read the Formspree Privacy Policy and security information.

Any applicable international data transfer must independently comply with Article 9 of the KVKK. This notice does not establish or guarantee that the required transfer mechanisms are in place; the site operator must separately verify the provider arrangements and lawful safeguards.

5. Retention and security

Messages may be stored in Formspree and the recipient's email service. They should be removed after handling and reasonable follow-up unless a legal retention obligation applies. Formspree's separate technical retention practices may also apply. The form does not request passwords, payment information, government ID numbers or special-category data.

6. Your rights

You can submit requests concerning your rights under Article 11 of the KVKK through the contact form. Some requests may require identity verification.

7. Changes

This notice will be updated if the form provider, cookies or data processing changes.
