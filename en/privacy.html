Privacy and cookies

Updated: 6 November 2026

This notice concerns Muhammet Demir’s personal website at must7y.github.io/muhammetdemir/. It does not describe the data handling of separate apps such as Pi-Mo or BUMBALO.

Website operator

The website is operated by Muhammet Demir. This notice explains known data practices; it is not a legal compliance certificate.

Visits and cookies

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Its own code does not set advertising or analytics cookies, create visitor profiles, or store language choices in localStorage. GitHub may process IP addresses and other technical information to operate and secure its service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Contact form status

The contact form is temporarily disabled. The website is not currently collecting new names, email addresses or messages through it. The form was paused because the applicable safeguards for international transfers under Türkiye’s KVKK have not yet been verified for the previous provider, Formspree. This notice will be updated before submissions resume.

Possible earlier submissions

If a visitor submitted a message while the form was previously enabled, the name, email address and message may have been processed by Formspree, Inc., stored in the Formspree account and forwarded to the configured email recipient. Formspree states that it uses AWS infrastructure in the United States. Its published free-plan message history is 30 days; email notifications and technical/security records may follow different retention periods. Whether any submissions were received or deleted must be checked in the Formspree account.

Formspree’s privacy policy and security information describe its practices. Formspree may also collect technical data such as IP addresses and browser details. The website does not request sensitive personal information or government ID numbers.

International transfers and legal review

Formspree’s published EU GDPR Standard Contractual Clauses do not automatically satisfy Türkiye’s KVKK Article 9 transfer conditions. Because the required legal basis and safeguards have not been confirmed, the form remains disabled. Technical data processing by the host and other providers also requires a case-specific assessment. This notice does not certify lawful international transfers.

Rights and requests

Your rights under Article 11 of the KVKK remain applicable. Formal requests follow Article 13 of the KVKK and the relevant Turkish procedural rules. A general contact form cannot automatically be treated as a formal privacy request mechanism without the required verification and other conditions. A dedicated suitable contact process has not yet been finalized, so full legal compliance cannot be claimed.

Changes

Before the contact form is reactivated, this notice will be updated to explain the contact mechanism, data categories, purposes, legal grounds, transfer safeguards and retention rules.
