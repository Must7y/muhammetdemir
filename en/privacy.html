Privacy and cookies

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This notice concerns Muhammet Demir's personal portfolio at must7y.github.io/muhammetdemir. It does not cover the data handling of any mobile games mentioned on the website.

1. Website owner and contact

The website is operated by Muhammet Demir. A contact form is being prepared for inquiries, including privacy requests. The form is not yet able to accept messages or requests. This notice will be updated once a contact channel is enabled.

2. Visiting the website

This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. Its own code does not provide user accounts, comments, a working form-submission service, advertising, analytics or behavioral tracking. The contact form displayed on the site is currently disabled. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses for security. Its processing is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

3. Cookies and local storage

The website's own code does not set cookies or save data to browser localStorage. Language selection uses ordinary links and is not stored in the browser. If analytics, advertising or other non-essential technologies are introduced, this notice will be updated and consent will be sought before activation wherever legally required. Hosting and browser services may carry out their own technical processing.

4. Contact form

The name, email and message fields are currently disabled. No information is transmitted or stored through those fields at this stage. Before submissions are enabled, this notice will explain the service provider, categories and purposes of processing, applicable legal grounds, retention periods and any international transfers. Appropriate safeguards will be assessed first.

5. Legal grounds and recipients

The site uses GitHub Pages for hosting. Technical data may be processed by the hosting provider to operate and secure the service. GitHub may use systems outside Türkiye. Any relevant international-transfer obligations require separate consideration under Article 9 of the KVKK. Legal grounds and recipients for any future form processing will be explained before the form is activated.

6. Rights and security

Your rights under Article 11 of the KVKK remain applicable. The contact form does not yet accept privacy requests; the contact process will be published here once activated. The site does not request passwords, national identification numbers or sensitive personal information. Please avoid sharing unnecessary sensitive data.

7. Changes

This notice will be reviewed if contact, analytics or hosting arrangements change. Mobile applications will have separate privacy disclosures when required.
