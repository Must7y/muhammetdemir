Privacy and cookies

Updated: 6 November 2026

This notice covers Muhammet Demir's personal site at must7y.github.io/muhammetdemir/. It does not cover the data practices of games or other applications described here.

Website operator

The website is operated by Muhammet Demir. For general inquiries or initial contact regarding your personal data, use the contact form.

Site visits and cookies

This is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. The site's own code does not run advertising, visitor analytics, marketing cookies or localStorage-based tracking. GitHub may process technical details such as IP addresses to provide and secure its service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Contact form and processing

When you submit the form, your email address and message are processed to receive and respond to your inquiry. Providing a name is optional. Information is not added to a marketing list. Formspree may also process IP address, browser details, access times and referring pages to provide security and spam controls. Please do not send passwords, identification numbers, health information or other sensitive personal data.

Responding to inquiries may rely on legitimate interests under Article 5(2)(f) of Türkiye's KVKK where the conditions are met. Other legal grounds may apply depending on the message. This notice is not a substitute for any consent separately required by law.

Service providers and international transfers

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. The form uses Formspree, Inc. Submissions go to Formspree, may be stored in its systems and may be forwarded to the email recipient configured in the account. Formspree states that its infrastructure is hosted by Amazon Web Services in the United States. Submitting a message may therefore transfer your data outside Türkiye. Please review Formspree's Privacy Policy and security statement.

The international-transfer safeguards required under Article 9 of Türkiye's KVKK have not yet been fully verified. The EU Standard Contractual Clauses described by Formspree do not automatically satisfy Türkiye-specific requirements. Publishing this notice is not proof of legal compliance; the relevant provider contracts and transfer mechanisms require further review.

Retention and security

Messages may be stored in the Formspree account and in the recipient's email service if notifications are enabled. Messages should be deleted when no longer needed for a response or reasonable follow-up unless a legal obligation applies. The service provider may retain technical records for different periods; actual retention and deletion settings require account review. Formspree may apply server-side spam filters.

Your rights

You retain your rights under Article 11 of the KVKK. You may start an inquiry through the contact form, but formal privacy requests remain subject to Article 13 and the relevant verification and submission methods; an ordinary message form is not necessarily a valid formal request channel.

Changes

This notice will be updated when the form provider, data practices or cookie use changes.
