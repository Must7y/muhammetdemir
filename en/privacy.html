Privacy and cookies

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This notice concerns Muhammet Demir's personal portfolio at must7y.github.io/muhammetdemir. It does not cover the data handling of any mobile games mentioned on the website.

1. Website owner and contact

The website is operated by Muhammet Demir. For privacy inquiries and requests about your personal information, contact Zekracore [at] gmail [dot] com.

2. Visiting the website

This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. Its own code does not provide user accounts, comments, online contact forms, advertising, analytics or behavioral tracking. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses for security. Its processing is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

3. Cookies and local storage

The website's own code does not set cookies or save data to browser localStorage. Language selection uses ordinary links and is not stored in the browser. If analytics, advertising or other non-essential technologies are introduced, this notice will be updated and consent will be sought before activation wherever legally required. Hosting and browser services may carry out their own technical processing.

4. Contact by email

If you email us, your email address, message and any personal information you include may be processed to read and respond to your request. Messages are not submitted through a web form; they may pass through and be stored by the email provider. Correspondence is kept only as long as needed for the communication, subject to legal retention obligations. Messages are not added to marketing lists.

5. Legal grounds and recipients

Where applicable, responding to inquiries may rely on the legitimate-interests or legal-claims conditions under Article 5 of Türkiye's personal data protection law (KVKK). Hosting uses GitHub and email uses Google's infrastructure. Their systems may operate outside Türkiye. Any relevant international data-transfer obligations require separate assessment under Article 9 of the KVKK; publishing this notice does not replace necessary safeguards.

6. Rights and security

You may contact the email above regarding your rights under Article 11 of the KVKK. The website does not request passwords, national identification numbers or sensitive personal information. Please do not include such information in ordinary emails.

7. Changes

This notice will be reviewed if contact, analytics or hosting arrangements change. Mobile applications will have separate privacy disclosures when required.
